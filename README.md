# Gloss

Gloss is a note app I made for myself, specifically for studying mathematics.

The app takes advantage of the natural strucure of a maths textbook and links together explanations, theorems, problems etc. so you can easily refer to other parts of the textbook without having to search through pages of text.

Each problem of an exam paper or each paragraph of a textbook can be clicked into and has its own canvas to write notes on. This way your notes stay organised and easy to find. I also included a latex/typst writing tab so you can easily make typed notes as well - the AI can also help with this. All notes can be exported. 

Additionally, I wired an AI chat interface into the app directly so that you don't have to screenshot your work and give it to the AI or type out your whole explanation everytime you need to ask the AI something. You can also wire a local AI through ollama if you'd like.

## Features

- Theorems, explanations, proofs linked to each other automatically
  - Including references like "According to 1.13" - you can click on '1.13' and it will bring up that part of the text
- Native ink support (Ie palm rejection, point predictions, etc)
- A separate ink canvas for each paragraph in a textbook, each question in an exam paper, and also you can write on the pages too.
- An in-built AI so that you can ask it to clarify something in your paragraph, and it can refer to your handwritten and typed work, with a choice of providers. You can also ask it to mark your work, and get feedback.
- You can privately sync all data across devices easily via syncthing by just sharing the gloss sync folder.
- Support for windows and android.
